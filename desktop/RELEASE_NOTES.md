sam-ui 0.5.0: effects you can actually direct. macOS 14 or newer, Apple Silicon (M1 or later); 16 GB of memory recommended, and needed for SAM 3. The browser demo needs WebGPU; it's tested in Chrome, Firefox and Safari. After Effects export needs AE MCP Vision v2.2.0.

- **Effects are clips on the timeline.** Each effect covers a range of frames and shows as a block in an Effects band under its layer, with a Background row for background effects. Add effect puts a 3 second clip at the playhead. Drag a clip to move it, drag an end to trim it, and stack clips so the upper one draws on top. Split at the playhead (S), Duplicate (D) and Move up or down (Alt+Up/Down) work on the selected clip, and a split's right half carries on the animation. Every clip edit is one Cmd-Z step. The background effect is now saved with the rest, so it no longer resets to Original on reload. Effects saved by 0.4.0 are moved over as full-length clips. (#81, #82)
- **Typed settings for every effect.** The selected clip's settings panel has sliders, colour pickers and style buttons, and edits redraw at once without re-tracking. Reset returns a clip to its style's defaults. (#81)
- **Outline, Glow and Scribble.** A new Outline effect for layers, with width, colour, opacity and softness. Glow and Scribble are its other styles; Scribble draws a wobbly, hand-drawn line that can redraw a few times a second. The background effect that used to be called Outline is now Edges. (#81, #82)
- **Blur and Pixelate controls.** Pixelate gets a block size, and on layers a feather. Background Blur gets a radius and a smoother blur that no longer wraps round the frame's edges. A new layer Blur, for blurring a face or a person, has radius and feather and doesn't bleed the background into the edge. (#82)
- **Replace background.** Swap the background for a colour, a two-colour gradient or your own picture (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 50 MB), with Cover or Contain fit and a feathered edge. Your tracked subjects stay sharp in front of it. A picture is kept in the app, and the export dialog warns if a clip's picture is missing. (#83)
- **Colour pop.** Everything outside the tracked subjects goes grey, with amount and contrast controls; the subjects keep their colour. (#83)
- **Spotlight controls.** Disc or ring, colour, size, ring thickness, and darkening outside the light. The old Spotlight styles look exactly as before. (#83)
- **Editable Title.** Type up to 3 lines of text, then pick its weight, size, colour, position and alignment, with a Fade, Rise or Pop entrance. By default it sits behind the tracked subjects, so people can walk in front of it. Text that would leave the frame is drawn smaller. The old fixed-copy background text is now called Text (demo). (#84)
- **Glitter, Sparkle and Dust.** Particles that come off a layer, drift out, twinkle and fade, from its edge, its inside or both. They follow a moving subject and look the same on every replay and in the export. (#85)
- **Stickers.** A star, heart, arrow, speech bubble, price tag, sparkle, crown or label, or your own picture, pinned to a layer and following it, with optional smoothing and a short label on the label, price tag and speech bubble. (#85)
- **Echo and Strobe.** Fading or even copies of a layer from earlier frames, drawn behind every tracked subject, with optional tint. (#85)
- **Effects no longer vanish after you try several.** Each effect used to keep its own graphics context for the whole session, so after trying a handful, Chrome dropped the oldest and those effects drew nothing in the preview or the export until a reload. They now let go of contexts they're not using, and rebuild one that was lost. (#78)
- **Updated drawing library.** pts 1.0, with its style cache kept in sync, so background text, Erase, Desaturate and Original keep drawing correctly. It also clears the 8 high-severity npm advisories that came with the old version's build tooling. Text (demo) now wraps its lines slightly differently. (#79)
- **Lane drag-select works again.** Dragging across a layer's frame lane selected only a few frames, because the row's own drag cancelled it. It now selects the whole span you drag over. (#80)

**Unsigned build:** macOS may ask you to approve the app in System Settings > Privacy & Security on first launch. See desktop/README.md for installation steps.
